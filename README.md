# 🚀 About The Project

CaseMap AI is an advanced LegalTech platform that transforms unstructured legal stories into structured legal insights using Artificial Intelligence.

Users can describe their legal problems naturally through text, voice, or uploaded documents.  
The system then:

- analyzes the legal context,
- extracts entities,
- identifies legal categories,
- structures the case,
- generates legal roadmaps,
- tracks hearings and deadlines,
- and provides AI-powered legal guidance.

This is **NOT** a chatbot.  
CaseMap AI is an intelligent legal decision-support system.
